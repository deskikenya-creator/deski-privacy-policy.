Deski Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Introduction

Deski is a digital assistant designed to help organisations respond to enquiries and manage service requests and complaints. This policy explains how information may be handled when you interact with Deski.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features in use, Deski may process your WhatsApp phone number, messages you send, and information you provide when making an enquiry, submitting a service request, or reporting a complaint.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to answer enquiries, respond to requests, manage complaints, provide customer support, and improve the service.

4. Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the chatbot, such as WhatsApp and its platform providers, and hosting or database providers where applicable. Information may also be shared with authorised staff of the organisation you contact when necessary to respond to your request.

5. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Information should only be retained for as long as necessary for its intended purpose or as required by applicable law. Actual retention depends on the systems and settings used by the organisation operating Deski.

6. Your Choices and Requests

Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information through the chatbot. To ask about your information, request access or deletion, or raise a privacy concern, contact the organisation you were communicating with and identify your request as a privacy enquiry.

7. Children's Privacy

Deski is not intended to knowingly collect children's personal information without appropriate authorisation and safeguards.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the service develops. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy enquiries about Deski, contact the developer or the organisation operating the chatbot through its official contact channels.
